# Case-study evidence method

The Project Hub and case-study pages use a conservative evidence model:

- implementation claims are limited to behaviour supported by source code,
  tests or maintained technical documentation;
- private implementations are labelled as private and are not presented as
  public repositories or live services;
- demonstrations use synthetic or representative data and state their
  boundaries in the page copy;
- concept work is labelled as concept or prototype rather than deployed
  software;
- interface evidence is published only as labelled, sanitised derivatives with
  captions describing what the image demonstrates;
- raw operational captures, private records, credentials and internal system
  details are never part of the public runtime;
- the production-planning calculator documents its assumptions and regression
  tests, and is explicitly not an operational forecast.

This method keeps public case-study content reviewable without exposing private
operational data or implying production acceptance that has not been verified.
